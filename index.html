Pregnancy companion for Android

Your pregnancy, with your clinic beside you

MomCare follows your baby's growth week by week, keeps your checklist, appointments and health readings in one place, and lets your care team see the snapshots you share.

How to install

Everything for the weeks ahead

Built around the care you already get from your clinic.

  • Weekly baby development

    See how your baby is growing each week, with size and weight for that stage.

  • Daily checklist

    A short to-do list for each day, with reminders so nothing slips by.

  • Appointments

    Upcoming and past visits with reminders, and a way to request a cancellation.

  • Snapshots

    Share bump and ultrasound photos and read comments from your care team.

  • Health tracker

    Log blood pressure, weight, fetal kicks and mood, and look back over recent entries.

  • Insights

    Short articles matched to your week and trimester, from checkups to body changes.

For clinics

A web dashboard for your care team

MomCare comes with a separate web dashboard for clinic admins and healthcare providers. The app and the dashboard share the same records, so the care team sees what patients choose to share.

Staff sign-in
  • Snapshots and health readings. Assigned doctors and clinic admins can see the photos and health logs their patients share.
  • Comments on snapshots. Doctors and admins reply to a patient's snapshot, and the comment appears in the app.
  • Appointments. The clinic manages patients' appointments and reviews their cancellation requests.
  • Limited to the clinic. Other patients cannot see a patient's snapshots, readings or appointments.

How to install

MomCare is installed from this page, not from the Google Play Store.

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Tap the Download button on this page to get the MomCare APK file.

  2. 2

    Allow this source

    When Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

  3. 3

    Open the file

    Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install.

  4. 4

    Sign up

    Open MomCare, create your account and enter your pregnancy details.

Play Protect may show a warning. Because MomCare is not from the Play Store, Android may warn you before installing. Only install MomCare from this page; do not install copies shared elsewhere.

MomCare supports but does not replace care from your clinic. This is a capstone project. Do not use it for emergencies.
