Privacy Policy

At MomCare, your privacy is our highest priority — especially given the sensitive nature of pregnancy and maternal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through your use of the App:

  • Account Information

    Name, email address, phone number, and password you provide on registration.

  • Health Information

    Due date, pregnancy week, LMP date, first-time pregnancy status, and clinic details.

  • Usage Data

    Pages visited, features used, time spent in the App, and interaction patterns.

  • Device Information

    Device type, operating system, app version, and unique device identifiers.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalise the MomCare experience for your stage of pregnancy
  • Display accurate week-by-week pregnancy insights and baby development updates
  • Send you appointment reminders and health check notifications
  • Improve and develop new features based on how users interact with the App
  • Ensure the security of your account and detect fraudulent activity
  • Communicate important updates, changes to our policies, or service announcements
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We will never sell your personal health information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

03 Sensitive Health Data

MomCare handles sensitive health information with the utmost care. All health-related data you provide — including due dates, pregnancy details, and clinic information — is:

  • Encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols
  • Stored in encrypted databases with access controls limited to essential personnel
  • Never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms
  • Retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or as required by law
  • Accessible only to you via your authenticated account

04 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the App (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics), bound by strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or government request
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of MomCare, our users, or the public
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice provided to you

05 Cookies and Tracking

We use limited analytics tools to understand how users interact with our App. This helps us improve performance and user experience. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. You may opt out of analytics collection through your device settings or by contacting us.

06 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. If you delete your account:

  • Your personal profile and health data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Anonymised, aggregated usage data may be retained for service improvement purposes
  • Some data may be retained longer if required by applicable law
  • You may request data export before deletion by contacting support

07 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to our processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at momscare.noreply@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

08 Children's Privacy

MomCare is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

09 Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication support for your account
  • Role-based access controls for our internal teams

While we take every precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable any available security features on your account.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the App or by email. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.

11 Uploaded Photos and Care Team Sharing

When you use the Snapshot feature to upload photos, that image — together with any health data recorded at the time of upload — is stored securely and associated with your account. This information is visible to:

  • You, at any time, through your own account
  • A healthcare provider assigned to your care within the App, solely for the purpose of monitoring your pregnancy and communicating with you about your Snapshot uploads

Uploaded photos and related comments are never shared with any other party, used for advertising, or made publicly visible. Deleting a photo removes it from your gallery; some records may be retained briefly as described in the Data Retention section above.

12 Contact & Data Controller

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • momscare.noreply@gmail.com
  • www.momscare.app/privacy
